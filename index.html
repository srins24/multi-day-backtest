Multi-Day Strategy Backtest

Moving-average crossover: long when the fast average is above the slow one, otherwise in cash. Trades at the close, with costs on each switch.

Data. The default series is a simulated price path (seed 7, 22% annual volatility, 750 days). It shows the engine working. It is not BGEO and is not a forecast. A real multi-day test needs daily closes: upload a CSV with two columns, date and close, at least 250 rows. The one-session BGEO trade file cannot support a multi-day test.

Method.
  1. Signal at close t: long if the fast average is above the slow average, otherwise flat.
  2. The position is entered at close t and earns the return from close t to t+1, so the signal never uses the return it trades on.
  3. Cost is charged on each switch in or out, in basis points of the position size.
  4. No short selling, leverage, dividends, or slippage beyond the cost input. Sharpe uses a zero risk-free rate and 252 trading days.
One pair of parameters tested on one history is prone to overfitting. Treat the output as a test of the rule, not as a recommendation. Not investment advice.
